# Tuangi (\*Austrovenus structburyi\*, NZ Cockle) estuarine abundance

Discrete counts of tuangi in intertidal estuaries of New Zealand

## Usage

``` r
data(tuangi)
```

## Format

\## \`tuangi\` A data frame with 764 rows and 11 columns:

- year:

  Year of survey

- lon, lat:

  Longitude and latitude of the sample

- count:

  Total tuangi counted in a 250 gram sediment core

- mud_pct:

  % of sediment that was muddy (\<63microns?) at the time of sampling

- tn_conc:

  total nitrogen concentration (mg/L) at the time of sampling

- toc_pct:

  total organic carbon content (%) at the time of sampling

- tp_conc:

  total phosphorus concentration (mg/L) at the time of sampling

- sst_min, sst_avg, sst_max:

  Min, mean, and max sea surface temperature (deg C) at the location of
  sampling over the 12 months preceding
