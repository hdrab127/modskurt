# \*Aonides trifida\* estuarine abundance

Discrete counts of aonides trifida in intertidal estuaries of New
Zealand

## Usage

``` r
data(aonides)
```

## Format

\## \`aonides\` A data frame with 251 rows and 7 columns:

- estu:

  Estuary where the count was observed

- year:

  Year of survey

- count:

  Total counted in a 250 gram sediment core

- mud_pct:

  % of sediment that was muddy (\<63microns?) at the time of sampling

- tn_conc:

  total nitrogen concentration (mg/L) at the time of sampling

- toc_pct:

  total organic carbon content (%) at the time of sampling

- tp_conc:

  total phosphorus concentration (mg/L) at the time of sampling
